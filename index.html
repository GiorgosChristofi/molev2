PRIVACY · SOVEREIGNTY · SELF-CUSTODY

Privacy was never supposed to be a feature.
It was supposed to be the default.

The modern internet inverted that idea. Identity became infrastructure. Activity became data. Data became a product. Every click, payment, movement and relationship became something to log, index and sell.

Monero took the opposite path.

No public balances.
No public transaction graph.
No permission required.

$MOLE exists as the meme of that idea.

Not a promise of utility.
Not another roadmap pretending to be a revolution.

A mascot for private money, cypherpunk culture, and the belief that financial privacy should not require an explanation.

No trace.
No spectacle.
No apology.
Private by default.
